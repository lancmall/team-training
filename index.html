1.11.1

Lancmall 是什么

What is Lancmall

Lancmall 是一家专注于服务巴基斯坦企业的中国采购服务平台，是巴基斯坦 LAMP 集团旗下的跨境采购服务平台，帮助巴基斯坦企业和个人从中国采购商品，并提供从采购、质检、仓储、物流到配送的一站式服务。

Lancmall is a China procurement service platform focused on serving Pakistani businesses. It is a cross-border procurement service platform under Pakistan's LAMP Group, helping Pakistani businesses and individuals procure goods from China, with one-stop services from procurement, quality inspection, warehousing, logistics to delivery.

核心区别（一句话记住） Core Difference (remember in one sentence)

其他平台是让客户自己去买，Lancmall 是帮客户买。客户在其他中国电商平台采购，需要自己找供应商、自己沟通（大部分供应商只说中文）、自己安排国际物流、自己处理清关、自己承担运输风险；Lancmall 把这些复杂环节全部处理掉。客户自己选品——在我们网站上找到想买的商品告诉我们即可，剩下的——找供应商、采购、质检、仓储、打包、国际物流、清关、本地配送——全部由 Lancmall 完成。

Other platforms let customers buy by themselves; Lancmall buys for customers. On other Chinese e-commerce platforms, customers must find suppliers, communicate (most suppliers only speak Chinese), arrange international logistics, handle customs, and bear shipping risks. Lancmall handles all these complex steps — customers select the products themselves (find what they want on our website and tell us), and the rest (sourcing, procurement, QC, warehousing, packing, international logistics, customs clearance, local delivery) is fully handled by Lancmall.

核心价值：让中国采购像本地采购一样简单。

Core Value: Make China procurement as simple as local procurement.

六大优势客户价值详解

Customer Value of Each Advantage

优势一：中国本地团队，专业采购

Advantage 1: Local China Team, Professional Procurement

  • 无语言障碍：中国团队直接用中文和供应商沟通，效率高，信息准确。
  • No language barrier: China team communicates with suppliers in Chinese, efficient and accurate.
  • 供应商筛选：团队通过采购、入库检查等实际情况甄别供应商水平，遇到骗子、交付不稳定、管理混乱的供应商，交付团队会给出更换供应商的建议，帮助客户避免损失。
  • Supplier screening: The team identifies supplier quality through actual procurement and inbound inspection; if a supplier is unreliable, the delivery team recommends switching to protect the customer.
  • 采购进度透明：从下单到入库，每个环节都有记录和反馈，客户随时知道采购进展。
  • Transparent progress: Every stage from order to warehouse is recorded and fed back to the customer.

优势二：巴基斯坦卢比直接支付

Advantage 2: Direct Payment in Pakistani Rupees

操作方式：客户将巴基斯坦卢比转到 Lancmall 公司对公账户（Habib Metropolitan Bank），在网站后台上传转账凭证，财务审核通过后，金额充值到客户账户余额，即可用余额支付采购订单和转运订单。

Operation: Customer transfers PKR to Lancmall's corporate account (Habib Metropolitan Bank), uploads the transfer voucher on the website backend; after finance approval, the amount is credited to the customer's balance for paying PO and TO orders.

  • 不需要自己兑换美元或人民币；不需要担心外汇管制问题；不需要通过私人渠道换汇，资金安全有保障；所有费用以卢比计价，采购决策更简单。
  • No need to exchange USD or RMB; no forex control worries; no private-channel exchange — funds are safe; all fees priced in PKR, simpler decisions.

优势三：发货前入库检验，质量可控

Advantage 3: Pre-Shipment Inbound Inspection

  • 外观检查：检查外包装是否完好，有无明显破损、变形、污渍。
  • Appearance: packaging intact, no damage/deformation/stains.
  • 数量核对：核对实际数量是否与订单一致（100件以上需额外支付增值服务费用）。
  • Quantity: verify against order (100+ pieces requires paid value-added service).
  • 规格确认：核对型号、颜色、尺寸等规格是否与订单一致。
  • Specification: model/color/size match the order.
  • 拍照留档：检验过程拍摄商品照片，上传到客户订单后台；照片发给客户确认后，才安排发货。
  • Photo documentation: photos uploaded to customer's order backend; shipping only after customer confirms.
  • 增值服务：如客户需要，还可提供视频拍摄、商品试用等增值服务，进一步确认商品质量与功能。
  • Value-added services: video shooting and product trial available on request for extra quality/function confirmation.

客户价值：把质量问题拦截在中国境内，在发货前解决，避免有问题的商品发到巴基斯坦后造成更大损失。

Customer value: quality issues are intercepted in China before shipping — no bigger losses after goods arrive in Pakistan.

优势四：中国仓库集货，既安全又省钱

Advantage 4: China Warehouse Consolidation

  • 降低物流成本：多个包裹合并成一个大包裹，去除多余纸箱重量；二次打包优化体积重量；防震包装减少破损。
  • Lower cost: merge packages into one, remove excess carton weight, re-pack to reduce volume/weight, add protective packing.
  • 降低丢失风险：只跟踪一个物流单号，丢失风险大大降低。
  • Lower loss risk: track one tracking number only.
  • 方便管理：客户只需关注一个合并后的包裹。
  • Easy management: only one consolidated package to track.
  • 运费可预估，成本可追踪：可按每公斤成本估算运费，运输时效更可预测，客户对全程有掌控感。
  • Estimable freight, trackable cost: estimate by per-kg cost; shipping timelines more predictable; customers stay in control.
  • 免费仓储期：商品到仓后有30天免费仓储期；超过免费仓储期，每天每立方商品收费 ￥10，按当时 Lancmall 内部资金换汇成本计算（普遍略高于银行显示的汇率）。
  • Free storage: 30-day free storage; after that, ¥10 per cubic meter per day, calculated at Lancmall's internal exchange rate (usually slightly higher than bank rate).

优势五：一站式服务，全程无忧

Advantage 5: One-Stop Service

客户在 Lancmall 网站自己选品并创建采购订单，剩下的采购、质检、仓储、打包、国际物流、清关、本地配送、离境前售后全部由 Lancmall 负责（支持客户使用自己的物流）。

Customers select products on the Lancmall website and create a purchase order. The rest — procurement, QC, warehousing, packing, international logistics, customs, local delivery, pre-departure after-sales — is handled by Lancmall (customers may also use their own logistics).

优势六：巴基斯坦本地团队，无障碍沟通

Advantage 6: Pakistan Local Team

  • 客户可以用乌尔都语或英语直接和巴基斯坦团队沟通；同一时区，问题得到及时响应；售后有保障，找得到人；本地团队让客户更信任。
  • Customers can communicate in Urdu or English; same time zone, timely response; after-sales guaranteed; higher trust from a local team.

计费规则（必记公式）

Billing Rule (memorize the formula)

计费重量 = 体积重 与 实际重量 取较大值
体积重 = 长(cm) × 宽(cm) × 高(cm) ÷ 转换系数（一般5000–6000，视物流公司略有差异）
举例：包裹实际重量5KG，体积50×40×30cm=60000cm³，体积重=60000÷5000=12KG → 计费重量取12KG。

Chargeable weight = the higher of volumetric weight and actual weight
Volumetric weight = L(cm) × W(cm) × H(cm) ÷ divisor (usually 5,000–6,000, varies by carrier)
Example: actual 5KG, volume 50×40×30=60,000cm³ → volumetric = 60,000÷5,000 = 12KG → charge 12KG.

Payment Rules

  • Pay freight before shipping: after customer initiates TO, Lancmall re-packs and updates freight; customer pays first, then Lancmall ships. Because international logistics is prepaid, and Lancmall earns nothing on logistics.
  • 无COD（货到付款）：Lancmall 不支持货到付款。原因：跨境交易涉及国际物流、清关多个环节，COD面临极大资金风险和坏账风险；供应商要求款到发货；物流行业惯例是预付款。

Cost awareness: purchase price isn't the only cost; high price doesn't mean low profit (low freight, high selling price); low price doesn't mean high profit (high freight, low selling price, high loss rate); count visible AND hidden costs in the total profit check before purchasing.

5.35.3

选品原则三：运输可行性及运输成本

Principle 3: Transport Feasibility & Cost

运输可行性及运输成本不能独立考量。判断商品是否适合运输，必须结合运输成本一起看。

Transport feasibility and cost must be considered together. Whether a product is worth shipping depends on both.

判断标准 Criteria

是否适合空运？看三件事：① 商品体积小、重量轻吗？② 平均每公斤（或每立方）的商品单价高吗？③ 商品数量多、价值高吗？单价高 + 轻小件 + 货值大 = 空运划算，因为运费占比低、分摊到每件商品上成本可接受。体积大、重量重、单价低的商品空运不划算，因为运费占比过高，利润被运费吃掉。

Suitable for air? Three checks: ① small volume & light weight? ② high unit value per KG (or per m³)? ③ large quantity & high total value? High value + light/small + large value = air works because freight share is low per unit. Bulky, heavy, low-value goods are NOT air-suitable — freight eats the profit.

很适合空运的商品品类

Categories Well-Suited for Air Freight

  • ① 高价值商品：珠宝首饰、手表、高端电子产品的周边配套用品——价值高，运费占比低。（注意：电子产品本身是敏感货，不要提倡推广）
  • ① High-value items: jewelry, watches, accessories of high-end electronics — high value, low freight share. (Note: electronics themselves are sensitive goods — do not promote)
  • ② 轻小件商品：服装配饰、美妆产品、小型电子配件——重量轻体积小，空运成本可控。原因不是价值高，而是体积小、重量轻、平均每公斤或每立方的商品单价高、商品数量高。
  • ② Light & small items: clothing accessories, beauty products, small electronic parts — light, small, controllable air cost. The reason is not high value but small volume, light weight, high value per KG/m³, and high quantity.
  • ③ 紧急需求商品：季节性商品、节日促销、断货补货——时效要求高，空运快速补货。
  • ③ Urgent needs: seasonal goods, holiday promos, restock — high time sensitivity, air restocks fast.
  • ④ 新品试销商品：小批量空运试错成本低，快速验证市场。
  • ④ New product trials: small batch air test at low cost, fast market validation.
  • ⑤ 有保质期的商品：食品、化妆品等——海运时间太长，必须赶时效走空运。
  • ⑤ Items with expiry dates: food, cosmetics — sea takes too long, must use air to beat expiry.
  • ⑥ 高周转热销商品：空运快速补货，避免断货损失，资金回笼快。
  • ⑥ Fast-moving hot sellers: air restocks quickly, avoids stockout loss, fast cash return.

5.45.4

选品原则四：供应商质量

Principle 4: Supplier Quality

供应商质量决定商品质量和售后服务。选择靠谱的供应商是选品的重要环节。

Supplier quality determines product quality and after-sales. Choosing a reliable supplier is key.

  • 供应商评分：查看供应商评分（建议选择评分超过4分的供应商）；评分低的供应商质量和售后风险高。
  • Rating: prefer suppliers rated above 4; low-rated suppliers carry high quality/after-sales risk.
  • 销量：查看商品月销量（建议选择月销量＞1000的商品）；销量高的商品质量通常更稳定。
  • Sales: prefer monthly sales > 1,000; high volume usually means stable quality.
  • 认证：选择有认证的商品（3C认证、CE认证等）；认证是商品质量的保障。
  • Certification: prefer certified goods (3C, CE, etc.); certification guarantees quality.
  • 价格：不要过分追求低价，一分价格一分货；选择价格合理的商品，避免过低价格带来的质量风险。选择有认证、价格不要过低的、供应商评分超过4分、且月销量＞1000的商品。
  • Price: don't chase the lowest price — you get what you pay for; choose reasonable prices to avoid quality risk. Pick certified goods, not too cheap, supplier rating > 4, monthly sales > 1,000.

注意：不要提"品牌产品"——品牌产品属于敏感货，运输受限。引导客户选择有认证、评分高、销量大、价格合理的商品，而不是品牌商品。

Note: do NOT mention "brand products" — branded goods are sensitive with shipping restrictions. Guide customers to certified, high-rated, high-volume, reasonably-priced goods, not branded items.

销售业绩核算规则（重点）

Sales Performance Calculation (Key)

自营销售业绩 = 完成履约的净销售额（Net Sales）
净销售额 = 客户实际完成订单并已履约交付的销售额。提成按完成履约的净销售额计算。不以充值金额计算，也不以广告平台订单数计算。

In-house sales performance = Net Sales after fulfillment
Net sales = sales of orders actually fulfilled and delivered. Commission is based on net sales after fulfillment. NOT based on top-up amount or ad platform order count.

  • 为什么按净销售额计算？充值金额不代表真实成交（客户可能充值后不消费）；广告平台订单数不代表Lancmall收入；只有订单完成履约、客户确认收货，才产生真实销售。
  • Why net sales? Top-up doesn't mean real deals (customers may not spend); ad platform order count doesn't reflect Lancmall revenue; only fulfilled & confirmed orders create real sales.
  • 退款和拒付的影响：发生退款或拒付时，该订单不计入销售业绩，并可能影响客户留存率和真实毛利率考核。
